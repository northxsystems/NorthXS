FAQ

Questions Business Owners Usually Ask.

What type of businesses is NorthX built for?

NorthX is built for local service businesses such as contractors, home service teams, trades, and appointment-based local companies that need to manage leads, customers, quotes, follow-up, and campaigns from one place.

Does NorthX replace my CRM?

For many small service businesses, NorthX can become the main customer and lead system. If you already use a CRM, NorthX can still help organize daily work, missed opportunities, follow-up, and customer activity around your operating workflow.

Can NorthX recover missed calls?

Yes. Missed-call recovery is one of the core workflows NorthX supports when phone and SMS setup is configured for your business.

Does NorthX include SMS?

Yes. Plans include a monthly SMS segment allowance. Additional usage can be handled separately or as an add-on depending on setup.

Are email campaigns included?

Email campaigns are part of the roadmap and are labeled as coming soon. Current campaign workflows focus on available lead, customer, follow-up, review, and SMS capabilities.

Can NorthX connect to Facebook or Instagram?

Social lead connections are planned. NorthX already supports website forms and customer workflows, and future integrations will be labeled clearly as they become available.

Is NorthX difficult to set up?

The goal is a guided setup. NorthX is designed for non-technical owners and office teams, with support for getting the core workflows connected.

Can my team use it?

Yes. NorthX is intended for owners, office staff, and team members who need visibility into leads, customers, quotes, and follow-up work.

Is there a contract?

The public pricing direction is no long-term contracts. Any usage limits, add-ons, and activation details should be reviewed clearly before setup.

Which features are available now versus coming soon?

Core operating workflows such as Overview, leads, customers, quote requests, timelines, SMS, missed-call recovery, and basic follow-up exist in the product. Email campaigns, deeper integrations, and the AI Business Manager are roadmap items and are labeled as future capabilities.
